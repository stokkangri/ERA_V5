Chronologically

Vanilla attention was not wrong. It was expensive. Everything after it is someone staring at that bill and trying to pay less of it.

What you cannot see from a list

Grouped by family, these look like competing recipes. Laid out by launch date, they look like a field changing its mind about which part of the bill hurts most.

First it wants exactness (pay n²). Then it wants memory bandwidth back at decode time. Then it wants length. Then memory again — because long context made the KV cache the villain a second time. Linear / delta methods keep trying to change the asymptotics entirely. Once you see the oscillation, you can guess what comes next: another compression of state, or another way to drop the positional scaffold that helped training but blocks extrapolation.

2017 — Exactness Softmax attention + positions. Correct and costly.
2019–23 — Memory MQA → GQA → MLA. Shrink the KV you reload every step.
2020–25 — Length Windows, RoPE/ALiBi, sinks, YaRN, DroPE. Survive past L.
2020–24 — Asymptotics Linear, delta, gated delta. Stop paying n² at all.
